How to trace

  • Set up: prop the phone 25–40 cm above the paper, camera facing down.
  • On iPad: tap Draw on screen with Apple Pencil on the start screen. The Pencil draws (pencil, liner, grey marker, eraser on the left); two fingers move and zoom; tap with two fingers to undo, three to redo. Select on the rail: draw a loop, then drag, scale or turn it. Layers: add, lock, blend, merge and reorder. Touch and hold to pick a colour. Pattern: a kaleidoscope (mandalas), a seamless repeat tile (grid, half-drop, brick, mirror, hex) or one of the 17 wallpaper symmetries, drawn live; save the tile for print, or add it to your prints to fill a garment.
  • Place the picture: drag, pinch and twist it onto the paper. Tap Move first if it is locked.
  • Lock it: tap Lock. Now a pinch zooms in on details and the picture stays lined up. Double-tap to zoom out.
  • Steps: in Values or AI outlines, tap a number at the bottom to jump to that step, lightest first. All shows everything.
  • Tools has the view (photo, outlines, values), grid, strobe, camera and flashlight. Tap Done or the picture to close it.
  • Grid: in Tools → Grid, put the grid on the photo. It sticks to the picture as you move, scale or turn it. Draw the same squares on your paper to keep proportions and angles right; Diagonals help with perspective.
  • Base: load your own croquis or flat block (front and back) in Tools → Base. It sits under everything, fixed to the paper. Head lines (8, 9 or 10) keep proportions the same across a line-up.
  • Flats: turn on the centre line, then Mirror: draw one half and your own lines appear flipped on the other side, live. Ghost keeps a faint copy of a finished sheet to draw the next look over.
  • Boards save your base, its place, head lines and guides, so every sheet of a collection matches. Your work is also saved on this phone as you go.
  • Export: photograph the finished sheet, drag the corners, and save clean lines as PNG or SVG, or use them as your next base.
  • Show (Tools → View): Clothes keeps only the garments of a street or runway photo, in every view; Pick lets you tap a single garment (a shirt, the trousers) to trace just that.
  • Croquis from photo pose (Tools → Base) draws a 9-head fashion figure in the pose of your photo and places it over it. Face guide marks the eye line, nose and mouth.
  • Check symmetry (Flats) compares both halves of your sheet: green matches, red is missing, orange has no partner. Time-lapse (Export) records your drawing as a short video.
  • Markers (Values) labels each step with a grey marker number from the photo's real tones; Sheen hatches the shine to keep white. Lock to paper (Camera) keeps the picture on the sheet if the phone or paper moves.
  • Hide and Opacity help you check your drawing against the paper. Hide leaves the grid and guides showing.

Photos stay on your phone. AI sketch uses the Informative Drawings model by Caroline Chan, Frédo Durand and Phillip Isola (MIT License). Clothes, pose and piece finding use Google MediaPipe models (Apache License 2.0). All run on your phone; models download once.
